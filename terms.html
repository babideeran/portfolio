Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

These terms apply to every commission I take. By paying a deposit or confirming a quote, you agree to them. If something is unclear, ask me before we start.

01Scope of work

Each job starts with a written brief covering what I will build, references, and the agreed price. Anything outside the brief is a new request and may change the price and the deadline.

02Payment

  • I accept PayPal, Wise and Robux.
  • Small jobs are paid in full before work starts, or on delivery if we've agreed that first.
  • Larger jobs are split into milestones, with a deposit before I begin.
  • Each milestone is paid before I send the next one.
  • Robux payments must account for Roblox's marketplace tax, which the client covers.

03Percentage / revenue share

I do not take a percentage of earnings unless the game is already released. If we agree on a revenue share, the percentage, duration and payout schedule will be written down before work begins.

04Timeline & communication

I give an estimated delivery date with each quote. I reply within 24 hours and send progress updates on larger projects. Delays caused by late replies, missing assets or changed requirements move the deadline accordingly.

05Revisions & bug fixes

The number of revisions is stated in the quote (usually 1 to 2). Fixing bugs in my own work is free for 14 days after delivery. Bugs caused by changes you or others make later, or by Roblox platform updates, are quoted separately.

06Ownership & credit

  • After full payment, the delivered scripts are yours to use in the agreed game.
  • Until full payment, all code remains mine and may not be used, published or resold.
  • I may show the finished work, without private data, in my portfolio unless you ask me not to before delivery.
  • Reselling or redistributing my scripts as a standalone product needs my written permission.

07Cancellation & refunds

If you cancel, you pay for the work completed so far. Deposits are non-refundable once work has started. If I can't deliver the agreed work, you get a refund for the part not delivered.

08Confidentiality

I keep your game details, assets and unreleased content private. I won't share your files or place links with anyone else without asking you first.

09Security & liability

I write systems with exploit protection in mind, but no script is fully exploit-proof. I'm not responsible for data loss, exploits or earnings loss caused by third-party plugins, other developers' code, or changes made after delivery. My liability is limited to the amount you paid for the job.

10Contact

Questions about these terms? Message me on Discord babideeran or on Roblox The_Babideeran.
